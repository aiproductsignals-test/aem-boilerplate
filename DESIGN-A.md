# Variant A — Tomorrow's version of the site you have today

> Delta against `DESIGN.md`. Everything not stated here is inherited unchanged:
> palette, typography, radii, elevation, components, voice, IA priorities.

## The bet

There isn't one. A is the brief in
`stardust/prototypes/birch-creek-homepage-html-improvements.md`, applied
exactly — no extras, no embellishment, no creative reach. The reaction it is
built for is *"yes, that's us, with the obvious fixes."*

## Composition

Captured IA and section sequence unchanged: utility topbar → sticky header →
hero → audited-measures band → tools → plans → answers → heritage → footer.
Splits stay asymmetric and alternating; the only structural move is the hero
split inverting so the ledger takes the wider column (improvement 4).

## All nine improvements applied

1. Gold splits into `gold` (dark grounds, unchanged) and `gold-deep` `#8A6410`
   (light grounds, 5.18 / 5.37 / 4.75:1 on paper / card / birch). Focus ring
   becomes `3px solid gold-deep` with 2px offset.
2. A disclosure control below 900px carrying the four primary links plus
   Español / Hmoob / Language assistance, and a skip link as the first focusable
   element.
3. The four-up stroke-icon tool grid becomes a ruled index; each tool's
   verification interval moves into a right-hand mono gutter so the intervals
   align into a column.
4. The hero split inverts — the ledger leads, and `$1,384` / `$486` /
   "saves $180" becomes the largest mono type on the page.
5. The birch lenticel motif rises to a perceptible weight in the hero and is
   reused as the section rule.
6. The flat 84px rhythm becomes two tiers: 64px on tools, plans and heritage;
   44px on the measures band and answers.
7. The 17-step ad-hoc scale collapses to the seven steps in `DESIGN.md`.
8. One repeated primary — "See what care costs" — at the hero, after the plan
   grid, and in the footer band. All 32 investigative labels survive.
9. Favicon derived from the mark, inlined as a `data:` URI.

## Ground ladder

Unchanged from the capture: pine → pine-deep → paper → birch → paper → pine →
paper. Gold stays a text-and-outline accent and sets no fill.
