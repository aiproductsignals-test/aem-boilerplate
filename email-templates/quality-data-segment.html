You compare plans on published performance rather than on a brochure. So here are the two figures we publish, with their measurement windows attached — and one we do not publish, said plainly.
What we publish
96.4% of claims processed within 14 days — 2025, audited.
3.2-day median decision on standard prior authorization requests.
All 412 active medical policies public, no login — the first regional plan to publish its full policy library that way, in 2019.
We do not publish physician-level outcome or complication data. We would rather tell you that than point you at a star icon with no methodology behind it. If that is the figure that would change your decision, reply and tell us.
