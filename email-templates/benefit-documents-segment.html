Three documents, all public, all current.
Open enrollment is a comparison exercise, and benefit summaries are the wrong unit to compare. These three are the ones that hold their shape: they say what is covered, on what criteria, at what rate.
What we publish
The full formulary — tier placements with their published clinical rationale.
The policy library — 412 active medical policies with policy numbers, effective dates, and revision history.
Machine-readable price files — published monthly, at contracted rates.
Preventive care is covered at no cost share on all three plans. Whether a specific service bills as preventive or diagnostic depends on the codes your provider submits — ask us to confirm a scenario in writing before you schedule.
