We publish the reasoning, not just the tier.
A tier on its own is an assertion. A tier with its clinical rationale attached is something you can weigh — and take to your physician. Every drug on our formulary carries both.
What we publish
Published clinical rationale behind every tier placement.
Therapeutic alternatives listed per drug, with the tier each one sits on.
Annual tier review, and 60 days’ notice before any change takes effect.
A formulary exception is a separate process with its own criteria. We will send those criteria in writing on request, and we will not tell you in advance how an exception will decide.
