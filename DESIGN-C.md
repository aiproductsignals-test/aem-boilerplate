## Moves cut — the fabrication guardrail

These are **published, dated** measures, not real-time feeds. Three of the
register's signature moves imply liveness this brand does not have and are cut:

| Cut | Why |
|---|---|
| live pulse dots on realtime data rows | implies a real-time feed |
| periodic refresh-sweep gradient | mirrors a data-refresh cadence this page does not have |
| top-of-page operational ticker | implies streaming operational changes |

Animating a published figure to its published value is honest; implying a stream
is fabricated content and fails the content-sourcing scan. The `pulse` and
`sweep` tokens are copied into `DESIGN-C.json` verbatim from the register
catalog for schema completeness — declared, not applied.
