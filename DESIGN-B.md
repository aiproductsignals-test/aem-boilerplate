# Variant B — What if the gold became a surface you stand on?

> Delta against `DESIGN.md`. Palette and typography are pinned and **unchanged**
> — no colour is added, none is removed. What moves is proportion.

## The captured trait

`brand-review.html § Tensions → T-color-imbalance`:

> `#C3922E` gold (accent) — **text and outline only**, never a fill. It never
> sets a button, a chip, or a ground. … For gold this is clearly deliberate
> rationing and probably the single most characteristic move in the palette.

Gold carries palette weight **3** against pine's 368 and card white's 221 — the
least-used colour on the page and the only one with a distinct identity. The
same rationing is why gold is only ever 12.5px eyebrow text, which is exactly
why it fails AA at 2.48–2.81:1. B resolves that failure by **inversion**: gold
stops being small text and becomes the thing the text sits on.

## The composition bet

The ground ladder is re-weighted, not extended.

| Ground | Captured | Variant B |
|---|---|---|
| pine `#1C4D42` | hero, heritage, buttons, links (weight 368) | hero and heritage **bookends only** — its weight stops flattening the page |
| pine-deep `#143B33` | topbar, measures band | topbar |
| **gold `#C3922E`** | **text and outline only, weight 3** | **the audited-measures band and the recommended-plan surface** |
| birch `#F3F1E8` | one section ground | the supporting section rhythm |
| paper `#FBFBF8` | body, header, footer | argument section grounds |
| card `#FFFFFF` | cards | unchanged — ledger, tool index, plan surfaces |

The plan grid re-composes around the gold recommended card rather than sitting
as three equal columns: the recommended plan takes the gold ground and the wider
column, the other two hold card white at equal width.

**Contrast on gold.** Body copy on gold is `ink #24322C` at **4.77:1** (AA).
Pine on gold is 3.41:1 — permitted only at ≥ 24px display sizes or as a border,
never for body copy. `gold-deep` is still declared for the eyebrows that remain
on paper, card and birch.

## Improvements

Applied: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 — as in variant A.

Improvement 1 (the gold AA failure) is resolved **structurally** rather than by
patching: promoting gold to a ground with ink over it clears AA by inversion.
`gold-deep` remains in the token set for the light-ground eyebrows B still has.

## Motion

None. B's entire argument is colour proportion and composition; adding motion
would blur the axis it differentiates on.

## Differentiation

- **vs A** — the ground ladder is re-weighted (gold promoted to two full
  surfaces, pine demoted to bookends); the plan grid is re-composed around the
  recommended card.
- **vs C** — B changes colour proportion and plan-grid composition and has no
  motion; C changes neither ground nor composition and is entirely a motion bet.
  Orthogonal axes, different amplified traits.
