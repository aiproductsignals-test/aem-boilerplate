Straight answers

The questions people actually call about.

Our answer library is written from real member service calls, updated monthly, and specific enough that you don't have to phone us to confirm it. Signed-in members see answers matched to their own plan and claims.

Browse all answers

How do I confirm a provider is in network before I book?

Check the directory listing's "verified on" date, or ask us for a written verification with a reference number — it takes one call and holds if the directory was wrong.

Why can the same procedure cost three different amounts?

Facility fees. A hospital outpatient department, an ambulatory surgery center, and an office bill the same procedure at different contracted rates. Our estimator shows all three.

What are your prior authorization criteria — the actual documents?

All 412 active medical policies are in the public policy library, each with a policy number, effective date, and revision history. Your provider can cite them when submitting.

Where does my deductible actually stand?

Your accumulator updates as claims finalize, with a claim-by-claim breakdown you can export. If your math and ours disagree, we'll reconcile it line by line.

About Birch Creek

Owned by the people we cover. Run from where they live.

Birch Creek Health began in 1963 when a cooperative of Chippewa Valley dairy farmers, machinists, and nurses pooled premiums to cover their own families. We're still a member-governed mutual: no outside shareholders, a board elected by members, and a headquarters on the Chippewa River in Eau Claire — not a P.O. box.

1963
Founded as the Chippewa Valley Health Cooperative, 412 charter families.
1988
Renamed Birch Creek Health; coverage expands across western Wisconsin.
2019
First regional plan to publish its full medical policy library without a login.
2026
184,000 members across 21 counties. Still one headquarters, still in Eau Claire.
